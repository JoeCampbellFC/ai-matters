## Focus areas

- Facility / credit agreements and term loan documentation
- Security documents (debentures, charges, guarantees)
- Conditions precedent checklists and satisfaction evidence
- Completion / drawdown records
- Intercreditor or subordination agreements, where multiple lenders are involved

## Suggested search queries

- "facility agreement"
- "credit agreement"
- "security agreement"
- "conditions precedent"
- "completion"
- "drawdown"

## What "complete" coverage looks like

- The facility structure: lender(s), borrower, amount, purpose, maturity
- Security granted and by whom
- Conditions precedent and whether/when they were satisfied
- Current status: in negotiation, signed, drawn, repaid, or in default

## Notes for the agent

Conditions-precedent checklists are often the clearest record of what
actually happened and when -- prioritize them when available. Distinguish
a facility that has been signed from one that has actually been drawn.
