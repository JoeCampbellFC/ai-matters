## Focus areas

- Pleadings (complaints, answers, counterclaims) and other court filings
- Case strategy memoranda and legal advice on the merits
- Discovery requests/responses and key evidentiary documents
- Settlement correspondence and offers
- Court orders, judgments, and rulings

## Suggested searches

- The filed complaint or initial pleading in this matter
- Answers, counterclaims, or other pleadings filed in response
- Case strategy memoranda or legal advice on the merits
- Settlement correspondence or offers exchanged between the parties
- Court orders, judgments, or rulings issued in this matter
- Discovery requests, responses, or key evidentiary documents

## What "complete" coverage looks like

- The claims/defenses at issue and the procedural posture (filed, in discovery, trial, appeal, settled)
- The principal parties, including opposing counsel where identifiable
- Key legal arguments and the firm's assessed position or strategy
- Current status and any deadlines or next procedural steps

## Notes for the agent

Clearly separate allegations (what one side claims) from established facts,
and separate proposed settlement terms from a finalized settlement. Court
orders and rulings are the most authoritative source for procedural status.
