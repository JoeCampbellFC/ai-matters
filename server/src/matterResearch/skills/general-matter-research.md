name: general-matter-research
description: Default research strategy for a matter whose type is unclear or doesn't fit a more specific playbook.
---

## Focus areas

- Matter opening / engagement documents that establish scope and parties
- Principal correspondence between the client and counterparties
- Any document that appears to be a final, signed, or otherwise authoritative version
- Status reports, chronologies, or summaries already present in the workspace

## Suggested search queries

- "engagement letter"
- "matter opening"
- "status report"
- "agreement"

## What "complete" coverage looks like

- Who the client and counterparties are, and their roles
- What the matter concerns and what the team was engaged to do
- The current status (open, closed, resolved, pending)
- At least one authoritative document supporting each of the above

## Notes for the agent

Prefer this playbook only when no more specific one clearly applies. If the
workspace profile or early search results reveal a clearer matter type,
prefer a more specific playbook on the next run.
