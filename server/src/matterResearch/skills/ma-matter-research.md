## Focus areas

- Engagement or retention letters defining the transaction scope
- Letters of intent / term sheets
- Share purchase or asset purchase agreements and their disclosure schedules
- Due diligence reports, memoranda, and data room indices
- Closing checklists and completion/closing documents
- Ancillary agreements (escrow, non-compete, employment, transition services)

## Suggested searches

- The executed share or asset purchase agreement, including signed copies and amendments
- The letter of intent or term sheet setting out the originally proposed deal terms
- Due diligence reports or memoranda prepared for this transaction
- The disclosure schedules attached to the purchase agreement
- The closing checklist or completion agenda for this transaction
- Ancillary agreements such as escrow, non-compete, or transition services agreements for this deal

## What "complete" coverage looks like

- The commercial structure of the transaction: parties, consideration, key terms
- Conditions precedent and closing mechanics
- Material representations, warranties, and indemnities
- Current deal status: signed, pending closing, closed, or terminated

## Notes for the agent

A final signed agreement is far more important than a routine status email,
even if the email is more recent. Distinguish the originally proposed deal
terms (in a term sheet or LOI) from the finally agreed terms (in the signed
agreement) -- do not conflate the two.
